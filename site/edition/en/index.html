01 Elsewhere

A country going digital, on tools made for somewhere else

  1. 2022Mauritania’s digital transition has a national agenda. Public services, connectivity and digital security are part of the programme. Going digital should also mean tools designed for the people who use them. Source
  2. 2025That transition is becoming practical: the Ministry announced online criminal-record access through Houwiyeti and Khidmati. Local services can be designed around local needs. Source
  3. TodayGoogle storage, Netflix, Spotify and Alexa solve different problems, but share one question: how well do they fit life in Mauritania? Recurring costs, regional support and language support need to be considered together. Source
  4. At homeProgress should reach the light switch, too. A light should respond in the house, even when the internet is interrupted. That is where Ark starts: local control for everyday routines. Source
